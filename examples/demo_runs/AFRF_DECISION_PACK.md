# AFRF decision pack

## Gavin Lake — decision-grade public policy screen

- 2,907 blocks / 5,663.66 ha
- 5,385,148.57 m³ production over 300 years
- 61/61 targets met
- net after harvest cost: approximately CAD 585.6M over 300 years
- annual net after cost: approximately CAD 1.95M/yr
- NPV 4%: approximately CAD 48.3M
- P00: 192 blocks, 175,533 m³, 315-day screen
- P01: 237 blocks, 176,512 m³, 355-day screen

## Knife Creek — HMZ-constrained GWM policy screen

- 160 blocks / 3,481.77 ha; 157 blocks scheduled
- 4-entry FVS-BC state library: 120 states, 96 transitions, 32 canonical states, 4 cycles
- compiled spatial dataset: 128 tracks, 79 treatments
- 1,306 scheduled entries over 300 years
- 574,458 m³ total production
- P00: 59 blocks, 172,832 m³
- production is zero after P11
- annual net after cost under the public envelope: approximately CAD 208k/yr
- NPV 4%: approximately CAD 30.4M

This run enforces the legal HMZ offer matrix. It closes cycles, but the canonical policy enters
a reserve cycle after about 120 years. That is a real model result, not a missing state
transition.

## Knife Creek — high-regime upper bound

- same 160 blocks / 157 scheduled
- 1,570 scheduled entries
- 1,329,309 m³ total production
- P00: 75,731 m³
- production continues through P29
- annual net after cost under the public envelope: approximately CAD 482k/yr
- NPV 4%: approximately CAD 18.0M

This run deliberately does **not** enforce the HMZ matrix. High, Moderate and Low are offered in
every group. It proves that the revised state graph can carry sustained entries, but it is an
upper-bound sensitivity, not a legal-compliance product and not an approved management plan.

The earlier `knife-structural` run remains as a comparison. The `knife-p00-operational` handoff
is from that earlier structural P00, not from either new policy screen.

## Decision

Gavin is ready for a public decision-grade review. Knife now has:

- a legal-HMZ-constrained policy screen that closes cycles but does not sustain yield; and
- a high-regime upper bound that sustains entries but is not HMZ-compliant.

It does not yet have a legal, sustained-yield GWM plan that can be compared with Gavin on equal
terms. The next model step is stand-specific state resolution or an accepted spatial clump rule;
it is not another dashboard layer.

Finance is a public MWP #3 Figure 7 screening envelope, not a budget forecast.
