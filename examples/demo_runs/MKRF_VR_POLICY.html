MKRF variable-retention policy screen

Status: public policy seed, not a fully re-optimized plan or an approved management plan.

Policy

The screen uses dispersed retention on the MKRF full estate:

  • retention basis: percent of pre-harvest basal area;
  • selection: Douglas-fir first, largest DBH first;
  • first-entry treatments: CC, VR10, VR20, VR30;
  • policy floor: 400 m² retained basal area per 10-year period;
  • retained trees remain for one rotation on the full managed block;
  • the first harvest transitions to a composite plantation + retained-DF track; the next final harvest counts that DF volume and value;
  • no residual area is moved to unmanaged.

Method

  • VRI supplies block TPH, basal area, species shares and age.
  • The rotation DBH shape uses the pinned MKRF 684 tree list.
  • The rotation+50 shape uses FAIB PSP CWHdm live Douglas-fir, age 140–180 (2,817 records on 13 sites), blended by profile age.
  • The retained cohort is selected from the right tail of the DF DBH distribution until the BA target is met.
  • The composite track is additive for the retained overstory and plantation; it is not a two-cohort competition/mortality model.
  • The first harvest uses aggregate product curves; the residual log-class ledger is bucked into sawlog butt/upper/pulp.

Limits

  • This is a policy seed schedule, not a fully re-optimized target search.
  • The matrix coastal grades D/F/H/I/J/U/X/Y are still aggregate-scaled; the sawlog butt/upper/pulp ledger is not yet mapped to those grades.
  • The older PSP anchor is a regional estimate from 13 sites.
  • The 400 m² floor is a scenario policy, not a legal requirement.
  • Composite retained-overstory growth is an additive approximation.
  • This is a public WS4 screen, not an approved AAC, budget or legal plan.
