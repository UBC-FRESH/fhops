Gavin Lake MDWR — block vs group decision brief

Status: decision gate, not a new model result

Date: 2026-11-20

Bottom line

The legal-order block screen is public. The legal-order group one-cut state is a diagnostic and does not replace it.

The group/block MDWR ratio is 1.10. That is a material gap, not a geometry error: the 0.1 ha minimum opening removes assigned area that the block screen continues to cut.

Legal-order basis

The 2007 Transition/Deep Snowpack GAR order sets:

  • 45 m²/ha in ICH and 40 m²/ha elsewhere;
  • Low 33%, Moderate 25%, High 20% per pass;
  • 120/160/200-year rotations with 3/4/5 patches;
  • 0.1–0.4 ha warm openings; other 0.1–0.7 ha in transition and 0.1–1.0 ha in deep; frost-prone 0.1–0.3 ha in transition and 0.1–0.4 ha in deep.

The block screen uses these parameters. The group layout uses a 0.7 ha maximum everywhere and does not yet apply the frost cap; in ICH/deep this is conservative on size. Earlier MWP Table 30 ordering and 40 m²/ha screens are superseded.

Group-state facts

Data-source check

The approved UWR boundary and stand-structure classes were downloaded from the BC Geographic Warehouse and clipped to Gavin:

  • UWR U-5-002 / dwl_9 Beaver Valley North: 2,726.4 ha;
  • High 697.9 ha, Moderate 1,013.2 ha, Low 1,015.3 ha.

The model estate is 2,799.3 ha, so about 71 ha needs reconciliation. The official class layer is the GWM 7 stand-structure layer, not the adjusted Appendix 9 compartments.

Frost-prone is defined by the legal order as medium, high or very high summer frost hazard. The Steen et al. 1990 table and a 5 m DEM are available; a preliminary screen is reported below.

Preliminary frost screen

A preliminary screen now applies the FRDA Report 157 Table 3 process to the existing group layout, using a 5 m DEM and a 500 m topographic position index. It is a screening diagnostic, not a field-verified legal map.

  • 540 assigned openings (186.4 ha) are frost-prone;
  • 291 assigned openings (142.2 ha) exceed the frost opening cap;
  • 36 assigned openings (19.1 ha) are warm-aspect cases that exceed the 0.4 ha cap;
  • 181 assigned openings (42.6 ha outside) are not fully inside the approved UWR;
  • 499 assigned openings (221.6 ha) fail at least one of the frost/opening-size or UWR tests.

The threshold sensitivity run still leaves at least 93.5 ha of assigned frost-cap exceedances, so this is not a threshold artefact.

Repaired layout

A clipped-and-capped repair of the existing layout is saved as a diagnostic. It clips every group to the UWR and splits oversized groups on a 10 m grid:

  • 27,110 groups; 2,722.4 ha;
  • 4,671 assigned openings; 1,386.4 ha;
  • zero cap violations and zero groups outside the UWR;
  • assigned area falls by 46.4 ha, mostly because 42.6 ha was outside the UWR.

This is a layout repair only. The cohort and regrown phase model have not been rerun on it, and the public group result below still refers to the old layout.

Interim group target diagnostic

There is no approved group target framework. As a diagnostic, the legal-order block MDWR mean period (20,634 m³) gives an interim even-flow band of 10,317 to 30,951 m³ per period, with a 1,000,000 m³ final-stock floor and 150,000 m² final-BA floor.

The group cohort final stock (1,141,775 m³) and final BA (184,534 m²) pass, but the 40-year schedule period range (4,180–62,626 m³) fails the interim even-flow band.

A horizon-aware per-block phase schedule was regrown at the shifted ages. It produces 10,204–30,937 m³ and puts 26 of 30 periods inside the interim band. The fixed-volume 30/30 result was a scheduling artefact, not a valid regrown cohort result.

The four late periods are only about 113 m³ below the 10,317 m³ floor. Moving the floor to 10,200 would be a 1.1% goalpost shift. The options are: keep the band and fail, approve a wider/asymmetric band, or extend the horizon/treatment schedule. I recommend keeping the band and the block policy screen until that is a policy decision.

Why the block screen stays public

  1. The group model is a legal-order cohort prototype. A preliminary frost screen and a clipped/capped layout repair now exist, but the repaired layout has no cohort/phase run and no target framework.
  2. The repair removes the 327 assigned opening-size violations and the 181 outside-UWR openings, but it loses 46.4 ha of assigned area and has not been rebalanced to pass targets. It is a layout diagnostic, not a result.
  3. The adjusted Appendix 9 compartment map is a scanned plan map, not a GIS layer, and has not been reconciled.
  4. The UWR boundary and stand-structure classes have been downloaded from BCGW and clipped to Gavin (2,726.4 ha). Independent legal review is still needed; the repaired layout uses that clip and has no residual outside-UWR groups.
  5. The group run has no policy target framework comparable to the block screen’s 9/37/15 status.
  6. The group result is 1.10× the MDWR block result on harvested volume.

Replacement gate

The group state can replace the block-level public result only after:

  • full UWR-consistent group coverage;
  • a modelled frost-compliant layout using the Steen et al. 1990 process;
  • full 40-year cohort state;
  • reconciled adjusted Appendix 9 compartments and independent UWR review;
  • group target framework;
  • a group-state public scenario small enough to use.

Decision

  • Keep gavin-mdwr as the public legal-order block screen.
  • Keep gavin-mdwr-group as a diagnostic prototype.
  • Do not present the 0.522M m³ group result as a replacement or compliance determination.
