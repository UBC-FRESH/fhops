Status: decision gate, not a new model result

Bottom line

The block-level MDWR screen remains the public policy result. The complete group state is a spatial diagnostic and does not replace it.

  • 34,512 groups on 1,443 blocks

  • initial BA 102,863 m²; final BA 255,737 m²

The public gavin-mdwr-group scenario now exposes the full 2,797.5 ha group-cohort state.

Why the block screen stays public

  1. The group model is still a schematic 10 m cell layout.
  2. Minimum group size, cohort regeneration, and windthrow are not modelled.
  3. The adjusted Appendix 9 compartment map has not been reconciled.
  4. The 2007 GAR order and current UWR boundary have not been independently reviewed.
  5. The group run has no policy target framework comparable to the block screen’s 13/37/11 status.
  6. The group state is 1.8 ha short of the MDWR and uses a VRI cross-sectional BA fit, not a plantation BA model.

Legal order conflict

The exact 2007 Transition and Deep Snowpack GAR order (Amendment TransDeep_Feb07_ord.pdf) was reviewed on 20 November 2026. It establishes parameters that differ from the MWP #3 Table 30 assumptions used by the current public screen:

  • GWM 6: group selection requires ≥45 m²/ha in ICH and ≥40 m²/ha elsewhere;
  • Table 2: Low 33% / 120-year rotation / 3 patches; Moderate 25% / 160 / 4; High 20% / 200 / 5;
  • GWM 10 / Table 3: openings 0.1–0.4 ha warm aspect, 0.1–0.7 ha other, and 0.1–0.3 ha on frost-prone sites.

The current screen uses the MWP #3 Table 30 mapping (High 33, Moderate 25, Low 20) and a 40 m²/ha gate everywhere. It is therefore not compliant with the 2007 GAR order and must not be presented as such.

A legal-order block sensitivity was run:

  • full estate: 4.179M m³ / 300 years, targets 9/37/15;
  • MDWR-only: 0.619M m³.

A first legal-order group rebuild was run with Low33/Moderate25/High20 and the 45/40 BA gate. It gives 0.530M m³, final standing yield 1,290,057 m³, and final BA 256,412 m² on 2,797.5 ha. However, 436 assigned groups / 19.9 ha remain below the 0.1 ha minimum opening, so the group result is not yet a legal-order implementation.

Replacement gate

The group state can replace the block-level public result only after all of the following are true:

  • full 2,799.3 ha group coverage;
  • reconciled adjusted Appendix 9 compartment boundaries;
  • independently reviewed 2007 GAR order and current UWR boundary;
  • minimum group size and 0.4/0.7 ha maximum enforced;
  • 40 m²/ha pre-harvest BA tested at every entry;
  • 3–5 cohort structure and plantation regeneration modelled;
  • group and block target frameworks compared on the same area and schedule;
  • a group-state public scenario whose geometry is small enough to use.

Decision

  • Keep gavin-mdwr as the public policy screen.
  • Keep gavin-mdwr-group as a diagnostic prototype.
  • Do not present the 0.533M m³ group result as a replacement or as a compliance-grade MDWR plan.
