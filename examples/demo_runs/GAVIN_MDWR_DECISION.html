Gavin Lake MDWR — block vs group decision brief

Status: decision gate, not a new model result

Date: 2026-11-20

Bottom line

The legal-order block screen is public. The legal-order group one-cut state is a diagnostic and does not replace it.

Result Harvested volume Area basis Status
Full-estate legal-order block screen 4.179M m³ / 300 yr 5,663.7 ha estate public policy screen
MDWR-only legal-order block screen 0.619M m³ / 300 yr 2,799.3 ha MDWR comparison baseline

The group/block MDWR ratio is 1.02. That is a material gap, not a geometry error: the 0.1 ha minimum opening removes assigned area that the block screen continues to cut.

Legal-order basis

The 2007 Transition/Deep Snowpack GAR order sets:

  • 45 m²/ha in ICH and 40 m²/ha elsewhere;
  • Low 33%, Moderate 25%, High 20% per pass;
  • 120/160/200-year rotations with 3/4/5 patches;
  • 0.1–0.4 ha warm openings, 0.1–0.7 ha other, 0.1–0.3 ha frost-prone.

The block and group screens now use these parameters. Earlier MWP Table 30 ordering and 40 m²/ha screens are superseded.

Group-state facts

  • 25,777 groups on 1,443 blocks;
  • 2,797.5 ha represented;
  • 3,954 assigned openings, all ≥0.1 ha;
  • 1,431.9 ha scheduled;
  • maximum opening 0.7 ha;
  • 12,223 accepted cohort entries;
  • final standing volume 1,172,437 m³;
  • initial BA 102,863 m²; final BA 190,573 m².

Why the block screen stays public

  1. The group model is a legal-order cohort prototype, but frost-prone classification and target framework are missing.
  2. Frost-prone 0.1–0.3 ha classification is not implemented.
  3. The adjusted Appendix 9 compartment map has not been reconciled.
  4. The 2007 GAR/UWR spatial boundary has not been independently reviewed.
  5. The group run has no policy target framework comparable to the block screen’s 9/37/15 status.
  6. The group result is 1.02× the MDWR block result on harvested volume.

Replacement gate

The group state can replace the block-level public result only after:

  • full 2,799.3 ha group coverage;
  • frost-prone classification;
  • full 40-year cohort state;
  • reviewed Appendix 9 and UWR boundaries;
  • group target framework;
  • a group-state public scenario small enough to use.

Decision

  • Keep gavin-mdwr as the public legal-order block screen.
  • Keep gavin-mdwr-group as a diagnostic prototype.
  • Do not present the 0.522M m³ group result as a replacement or compliance determination.
