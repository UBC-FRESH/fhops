# Gavin Lake MDWR legal-order group cohort prototype

This is the **old-layout** group diagnostic, not a policy scenario and not the
current frost/UWR-repaired layout.

- 25,759 groups on 1,443 blocks; 2,797.5 ha represented.
- 10,182 scheduled groups; 3,947 assigned groups; 21,812 standing groups.
- Harvested volume: 679,174 m³ / 300 years.
- Final standing volume: 1,141,775 m³.
- Final BA: 184,534 m².

The current repaired layout is 27,110 groups / 2,722.4 ha, with 4,671 assigned
openings / 1,386.4 ha and zero frost/UWR cap violations. Its entry-offset phase
is 0.580M m³ / 26-of-30, with a 3,474 m³ tail minimum. The tail limit is
accepted; the block-level policy screen remains public.

Limits: schematic 10 m groups, old-layout cohort prototype, no windthrow, and a
VRI cross-sectional BA fit. Not a replacement for the block-level policy
screen.
