# Gavin Lake MDWR complete group-state prototype

This is a group-level WS4 prototype, not a policy scenario.

The complete group state covers 1,443 blocks and 20,139 groups;
9,674 groups are assigned to cuts and 10,465 remain standing.

Harvested volume: 532,827 m³ / 300 years.
Final standing yield: 1,185,395 m³.
Initial BA: 96,637 m²; final BA: 205,095 m².

Limits: VRI cross-sectional BA fit, base CC successor curves,
schematic 10 m groups, no minimum group size, no windthrow.
Not a replacement for the block-level policy screen.
