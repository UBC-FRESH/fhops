Correction found

Policy result

The published scenario is a policy seed, not a fully re-optimized target search:

  • 400 m² retained BA per 10-year period;
  • CC / VR10 / VR20 / VR30 choice grid;
  • full managed area;
  • retained DF carried in the composite next-rotation track;
  • no residual area moved to unmanaged.

All ten retention targets are met. The two remaining misses are even-flow yield checks; one even-flow check is above the band.

200-year next-rotation validation

To exercise the retained-DF state, the 100-year seed schedule was extended with second and third entries at +80/+160 years, capped at 200 years. The same matrix and 400 m² first-rotation policy were used.

Scenario Gross log value Production Net revenue Targets
200-year retention CAD 1,769.8M 8.319M m³ CAD 1,001.3M 68/13/6
200-year no retention CAD 1,739.4M 8.178M m³ CAD 983.1M 63/19/5

The retained-DF composite track adds about 140,434 m³ and CAD 30.3M gross over the two rotations relative to the no-retention schedule. The second rotation harvest is concentrated at offsets 80 and 160, as expected from the manual 80-year re-entry.

This validates that the retained cohort is carried into the next final-felling ledger. It does not validate a 200-year optimized schedule; the extension is a manual sensitivity.

Remaining limits

  • Coastal grade products D/F/H/I/J/U/X/Y are still aggregate-scaled.
  • The bucked ledger carries sawlog butt/upper/pulp, not a calibrated coastal grade mapping.
  • The composite retained-overstory curve is additive, not a two-cohort competition/mortality model.
  • The R+50 anchor is a regional PSP estimate from 13 sites.
  • The 400 m² floor is a scenario policy, not a legal requirement.
  • This is a public WS4 screen, not an approved AAC, budget or legal plan.

Conclusion

The public scenario is suitable as a draft retention policy screen. It is not yet a decision-grade coastal-grade implementation.
