Legal order

The 2007 Transition and Deep Snowpack GAR order sets:

  • group-selection basal area: 45 m²/ha in ICH, 40 m²/ha elsewhere;
  • Low: 33% per pass, 120-year rotation, 3 patches;
  • Moderate: 25%, 160-year rotation, 4 patches;
  • High: 20% per pass, 200-year rotation, 5 patches;
  • openings: 0.1–0.4 ha warm aspect; 0.1–0.7 ha other in transition and 0.1–1.0 ha in deep; 0.1–0.3 ha frost-prone in transition and 0.1–0.4 ha in deep.

These differ from the MWP #3 Table 30 ordering used by earlier versions of this screen. The current public block and legal group screens use the GAR order.

Limits

  • The adjusted Appendix 9 compartment map has not been reconciled with the fine BC long-term objective polygons.
  • The UWR boundary and stand-structure classes were downloaded from BCGW and clipped to Gavin (2,726.4 ha), but independent legal review is still needed. The old group estate is 2,797.5 ha, and 181 assigned openings have 42.6 ha outside the authoritative clip, so the boundary/layout difference must be reconciled.
  • The frost screen is preliminary: it uses a TPI proxy, not the field air-photo procedure, and no field verification or legal determination.
  • The clipped/capped layout is a repair of the old schematic layout, not a fresh design. Its pass fractions were not rebalanced; a cohort state has been run, but no regrown phase result exists for it.
  • The block screen does not lay out spatial groups.
  • The group screen does not model cohort regeneration, windthrow, or a plantation-specific BA model.
  • No public AFRF private finance ledger is used or fabricated.

Decision boundary

The block screen remains public. The group screen is a diagnostic. Replacing the block screen requires a reconciled Appendix 9 compartment map, an independent UWR review, a frost-compliant cohort that cuts only assigned openings, a regrown phase result, and a comparable target framework.
