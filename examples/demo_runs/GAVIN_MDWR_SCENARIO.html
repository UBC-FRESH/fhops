Legal order

The 2007 Transition and Deep Snowpack GAR order sets:

  • group-selection basal area: 45 m²/ha in ICH, 40 m²/ha elsewhere;
  • Low: 33% per pass, 120-year rotation, 3 patches;
  • Moderate: 25%, 160-year rotation, 4 patches;
  • High: 20% per pass, 200-year rotation, 5 patches;
  • openings: 0.1–0.4 ha warm aspect, 0.1–0.7 ha other, and 0.1–0.3 ha on frost-prone sites.

These differ from the MWP #3 Table 30 ordering used by earlier versions of this screen. The current public block and legal group screens use the GAR order.

Limits

  • The adjusted Appendix 9 compartment map has not been reconciled with the fine BC long-term objective polygons.
  • The 2007 GAR order and current UWR boundary still need a spatial boundary review.
  • The block screen does not lay out spatial groups.
  • The group screen does not model cohort regeneration, windthrow, or a plantation-specific BA model.
  • No public AFRF private finance ledger is used or fabricated.

Decision boundary

The block screen remains public. The group screen is a diagnostic. Replacing the block screen requires a reviewed Appendix 9 map, a reviewed UWR boundary, frost-prone classification, full cohort dynamics, and a comparable target framework.
