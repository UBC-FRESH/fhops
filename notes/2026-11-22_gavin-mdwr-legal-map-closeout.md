## UWR boundary review

- The U-5-002 order PDF contains the order text but not Schedule A; Schedule A
  is the spatial layer, not an embedded map.
- The BCGW approved layer identifies **U-5-002 / dwl_9 Beaver Valley North**,
  approved **2004-12-13**, at **2,726.42 ha**.
- The layer intersects **656 Gavin blocks**.
- The old group layout had **181 assigned openings with 42.58 ha outside** the
  approved UWR.
- The repaired layout has **zero groups outside** the UWR and covers
  **2,722.37 ha (99.85%)**. The 4.05 ha residual is dropped small pieces and
  edge residue.

## Appendix 9 review

- Appendix 9 page 149 is legible and contains the BV01–BV09 compartments,
  MDWR habitat colours, and the gross/net area table above.
- It has a scale bar and north arrow but no coordinate grid or georeferencing
  control points. It is a scanned plan map, not a GIS layer.
- The adjusted compartment boundaries therefore remain unreconciled to the
  official class polygons.
- The official stand-structure class layer is digital and sums to the UWR
  area: High 697.92 ha, Moderate 1,013.18 ha, Low 1,015.33 ha.
- The legal order's class rules can be applied with the official layer; the
  adjusted Appendix 9 compartment map remains a plan-alignment issue.

## Findings

1. The public UWR figure should be **2,726.42 ha**, not the old **2,846 ha**
   Appendix 9 gross figure.
2. The MDWR-only block baseline uses the **2,799.3 ha model estate**, not the
   approved UWR. It is a model comparison, not a UWR-exact legal baseline.
3. The block policy screen applies no-clearcut to the model MDWR estate,
   including about **72.9 ha** outside the approved UWR. That is conservative,
   but it is not an exact UWR implementation.
4. The repaired group layout is the only Gavin product clipped exactly to the
   approved UWR.
5. No legal-order compliance claim is made. The adjusted Appendix 9
   compartments remain a scanned plan, and the frost screen remains
   preliminary.

## Consequence

No model rerun is required for the current public position:

- block screen remains public;
- group screens remain diagnostics;
- the portal card now uses the 2,726 ha UWR figure;
- the model-estate 2,799.3 ha baseline is clearly labelled as a model estate.

A compliance-grade claim would require either adopting the approved UWR as the
legal boundary and rebuilding the block baseline on it, or georeferencing and
reconciling the adjusted Appendix 9 compartments. Neither is done here.
