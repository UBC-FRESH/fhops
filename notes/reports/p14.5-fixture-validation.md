## Fixture

Seven clipped VRI polygons in the TFL 6 pilot AOI (about 100 ha), one 20 m landing, one synthetic
road job, and seven ground-based machines. Native 1 m slope/CHM support rows are committed for
each block and the landing/road context. Synthetic work volumes, machine rates, landing capacity,
and road values are labelled in `provenance.json`; they are not field inventory or calibrated
costs.

## Operability

- `T6_23339461` (51.55%) and `T6_16625195` (40.47%) classify as cable; the fixture's
  `ground_fb_skid` assignment is reported incompatible.
- The other five blocks classify as ground and are compatible.
- Missing roughness rows produce the explicit `class_average_roughness` fallback label.
- Landing and cost context remain advisory; the fixture does not silently rewrite systems or apply
  financial multipliers.
